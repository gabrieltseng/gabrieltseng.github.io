We release v1.3, an embedding model. We also release precomputed global embeddings ([released on Source Cooperative](https://source.coop/olmoearth/olmoearth-v1-3-embeddings)) and a [powerful UI to explore them](https://olmoearth.allenai.org/embeddings). See our [tech report](https://allenai.org/papers/olmoearth-v1-3) and [interactive blogpost](https://narrative.allen.ai/olmoearth-embeddings) to learn more.
